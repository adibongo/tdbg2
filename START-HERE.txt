Turning Data into Business Growth — course website
Lagos Business School, Pan-Atlantic University

TO OPEN: double-click  index.html  in this folder.

That is the whole thing. It runs from your hard drive with no internet
connection and no server. Every page, all five facilitator decks and all
twelve datasets are inside this folder.

Keep the folder together. index.html loads everything else from the
folders beside it (assets, sessions, slides, data), so if you move
index.html out on its own it will open unstyled or blank.

The bongo.ai button in the bottom-right corner will say it is not yet
configured. That is expected: the assistant needs its small Cloudflare
service deployed first, which takes about five minutes and is described
in chat-api/README.md in the source repository.
